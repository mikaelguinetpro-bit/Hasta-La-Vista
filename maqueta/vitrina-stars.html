      Visibility

      AI Journal

      Everything the AI does appears here, live, with its reason. Nothing is decided in the shadows: the AI proposes, people decide.

      • Visibility: show every action and its reason.
      • Kindness: protect those who dare; never mock.
      • Optimisation: every resource in its rightful place.
      • Availability: open to everyone, as much as possible.

      • The Triad of Kindness

        A moderation decision only applies if it passes all three stages:
        1. The AI proposes. It analyses, flags and explains why. It never decides alone.
        2. The creators validate. The community takes care of its own space.
        3. The caretaker team decides. Professionals working together have the final word, with kindness.
      • Archive, don’t delete: what the Triad removes is compressed and archived; it can be recovered if there was a mistake.
      • Tell the author: they are always told and can explain themselves. They are a creator too.
      • Your data is yours: what you delete is truly deleted, instantly, with no Triad and no waiting.
      • Your name is the one you choose: no country, no ID. Just your spark.
