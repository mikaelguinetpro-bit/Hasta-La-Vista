01 — The intuition

Crowdfunding can be much more than giving money.

For us, beauty is at the heart of it all: beauty in what we imagine, in how we work together and in what we leave to the world. What if you could also contribute an idea, a skill, a few hours of work, a song, a machine or an AI agent? Hasta La Vista wants to connect all these ways of contributing, to carry extraordinary projects from desire to reality.

05 — The vision

Paying tribute
to beauty.

We want ambitious ideas to find their people, their means and their moment. We start with a question: what could we create together to make Spain — and the world — a little more beautiful?

And the platform itself will grow with that same energy: the community will be able to propose improvements, while a caretaking team selects them, credits them and protects the beauty and coherence of the project.

Out of great pain, something immense can be born.

This idea was born from my own suffering and from a heavy boredom. With an idea, my energy and a €10 ChatGPT subscription, I started building it.
